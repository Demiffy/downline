DOWNLINE
========

A co-op horror scavenger. Ride the Bureau's train down the line, strip dead
facilities for scrap and get back aboard before the dark gets you.

RUNNING IT
- Unzip anywhere and run Downline.exe. Nothing to install.
- Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" (the exe isn't signed):
  click "More info", then "Run anyway".
- Allow it through the firewall (private networks) and allow the microphone
  if Windows asks. Microphone: Settings > Privacy > Microphone > let desktop
  apps access your microphone.

PLAYING TOGETHER
- Everyone must run the SAME Downline.exe (different builds will desync).
- Host: type your name, pick HOST A CREW (port 24565 by default). To play over
  the internet, forward UDP 24565 on your router to your PC and give your
  friends your public IP. Press START THE SHIFT when everyone is in.
- Join: type your name and the host's address (1.2.3.4 or 1.2.3.4:24565),
  then JOIN A CREW.
- Voice is open mic by default. Esc > SETTINGS > AUDIO has push-to-talk (V),
  mic level, voice volume and the open-mic gate.

THE LOOP
1. Use the terminal at the front of the train ([E]) and type LEAVE_CHECKPOINT.
2. MAP, VIEW_STATION B, ROUTE_TO B. Each assignment is 3 stations, then the
   checkpoint. HELP lists every command.
3. At a station follow the ELEVATOR signs, ride down, find scrap and carry it
   back INSIDE the train. The train leaves at 24:00, with or without you.
4. After 3 stations, ROUTE_TO CHECKPOINT. Throw scrap into the shredder, pull
   the lever and meet the quota, or the train rams the gate.

CONTROLS
WASD / Shift / Ctrl / Space   move / sprint / crouch (silent) / jump
E (hold for some)             pick up, use, ride the lift, pry crates
LMB / RMB                     use held item
F or middle mouse             scan (needs the Scanner)
1-6, mouse wheel              select slot
Q / C                         drop item / drop equipment
V                             push-to-talk (if set)
Esc                           menu and settings
F11 or Alt+Enter              fullscreen

Settings and saves live in %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Downline\
